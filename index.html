Tephra Rising

A free fantasy hero-battle game for iPhone

Pick a hero, learn their skills and ultimate, and fight in four modes: lane duels, real-time strategy, a four-castle free-for-all and a room-by-room dungeon. Play against computer opponents; no account and no internet needed.

How to play

  • Move: drag the left thumb stick.
  • Attack: hold the big sword button; it targets the nearest enemy.
  • Skills Q, W, E and ultimate R: tap to cast, or drag away from the button to aim; slide back onto the cancel circle to cancel. Skills show their exact shape on the ground while you aim.
  • Duel: lead your hero in a lane battle, build mercenary camps, level up and break the enemy base.
  • RTS: gather gold, build a base and command an army, 1v1 or Siege.
  • Soul Wars: four castles, three heroes each. Slay monsters for ichor, bank it at the central obelisk, beat the Ash Dragon and be the last castle standing.
  • Dungeon: dodge telegraphed attacks, pick rewards, choose your doors and beat the bosses.
  • Settings (main menu): sound volume, screen shake, graphics detail (Low, Medium, High) and the credits.

Questions

Is it really free? Yes. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no account.

Does it need internet? No. It works fully offline.

The game runs hot or choppy. Open Settings on the main menu and set Graphics detail to Medium or Low.
