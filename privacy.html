Privacy Policy

Tephra Rising · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Tephra Rising has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no servers of its own. The developer does not collect, receive or sell your information.

What stays on your iPhone

Your settings (sound volume, screen shake, graphics detail) and any unlocks stay on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network

The game makes no network requests and works fully offline.

Permissions

The game does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
